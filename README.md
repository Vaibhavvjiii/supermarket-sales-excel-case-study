# Supermarket Sales Excel Case Study

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a supermarket sales dataset to understand sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and sales trends.

The analysis was performed using WPS Office Spreadsheet with PivotTables, PivotCharts, formulas, data-quality checks, and dashboard reporting.

## Business Objective

The objective was to analyze supermarket sales data and identify important business patterns that could support better decision-making.

## Tools Used

- WPS Office Spreadsheet
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Excel/Spreadsheet formulas
- Data cleaning
- Data-quality analysis
- Dashboard design

## Data Quality Checks

The dataset was inspected for:

- Duplicate records
- Blank values
- NULL values
- Incomplete entries
- Data-type issues
- Text consistency

## Analysis Performed

Five main analytical areas were created:

1. Sales & Profit by City
2. Sales & Profit by Customer Type
3. Sales & Profit by Product Type
4. Monthly Sales Trend
5. Weekly Sales Trend

Additional product and KPI analysis was also performed.

## Dashboard KPIs

- Total Sales: $322,966.75
- Total Profit: $15,379.37
- Profit Margin: 4.76%
- Total Orders: 1,000

## Key Business Insights

- Naypyitaw generated the highest sales at $110,568.71 and the highest profit at $5,265.18 among the three cities.
- Food and Beverages generated the highest sales at $56,144.84 and the highest profit at $2,673.56 among the product types.
- Members generated higher sales and profit than Normal customers.
- Sales declined from January ($116,291.87) to February ($97,219.37), then recovered in March ($109,455.51).
- Weekly sales showed noticeable fluctuations that could be investigated further.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate the factors driving strong sales and profit performance in Naypyitaw and determine whether successful practices can be applied to other cities.
- Maintain availability of Food and Beverages and investigate opportunities to grow this product type further.
- Analyze successful Member customer behavior and identify strategies to increase conversion of Normal customers into Members.
- Investigate the reasons behind weekly and monthly sales fluctuations.
